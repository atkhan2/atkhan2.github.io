<h2>Plot 1</h2>
<iframe src="/plot1.html" width="700" height="500"></iframe>

<p>
This chart shows the number of Bigfoot sightings per month. The x-axis is the month and the y-axis is the number of sightings. I used a bar chart because it is an easy way to compare the number of sightings across the months. I converted the date column into a datetime format and extracted the month so I could group the data easier.
</p>

<h2>Plot 2</h2>
<iframe src="/plot2.html" width="700" height="500"></iframe>

<p>
This chart shows the locations of Bigfoot sightings using longitude and latitude coordinates. I used a scatterplot because it was the best option for showing the different points of sightings. I colored the points to make the chart easier to read. For the interactivity aspect, I added a legend that lets the user click to highlight certain states.
</p>
